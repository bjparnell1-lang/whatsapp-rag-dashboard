[4/10/26, 4:24:22 PM] Dr. Bernadette Sanko Kamara Inc: For a behavioral health intake that includes **Substance Use Disorder (SUD)** clients, the goal is to conduct a holistic assessment that captures not just the clinical symptoms, but the social and environmental context of the individual.
A standard intake generally follows these key domains:
### 1. Presenting Problem & History
 * **Chief Complaint:** "What brings you in today, and what are your primary goals for treatment?"
 * **History of Presenting Illness:** When did these symptoms or usage patterns start? Have they changed recently?
 * **Psychiatric History:** Previous diagnoses, hospitalizations, or outpatient treatments.
### 2. Substance Use History (SUD Specific)
 * **Usage Patterns:** "What substances are you currently using? How often, how much, and what is the route of administration (oral, inhalation, injection)?"
 * **Onset:** "At what age did you first use [Substance], and when did it become a regular habit?"
 * **Withdrawal & Tolerance:** "Have you ever experienced tremors, seizures, or hallucinations when trying to stop? Do you find you need more to get the same effect?"
 * **Impact:** "How has your use affected your work, relationships, or legal status?"
### 3. Medical & Physical Health
 * **Chronic Conditions:** Assessing for HIV, Hepatitis C, diabetes, or hypertension (common comorbidities).
 * **Medications:** Current prescriptions and any history of Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) like Buprenorphine or Methadone.
 * **Sleep & Nutrition:** Changes in appetite or sleep architecture.
### 4. Risk Assessment
 * **Suicidality/Homicidality:** "Are you currently having thoughts of harming yourself or others?"
 * **Safety:** Assessing for domestic violence or unsafe living environments.
 * **Overdose History:** "Have you ever experienced a non-fatal overdose? Do you have access to Narcan?"
### 5. Psychosocial & Environmental Factors
 * **Living Situation:** "Is your current housing stable? Does anyone you live with also use substances?"
 * **Employment & Education:** "Are you currently working or in school?"
 * **Support System:** "Who can you count on for emotional or practical support during this process?"
 * **Legal History:** Any pending court dates, probation, or parole requirements.
### 6. Mental Status Exam (MSE)
While the client talks, the clinician observes:
 * **Appearance:** Hygiene, attire, and physical marks (track marks, etc.).
 * **Affect/Mood:** Is the client anxious, depressed, or irritable?
 * **Cognition:** Memory, orientation (time/place), and thought organization.
### 7. Readiness to Change
 * **Stage of Change:** Is the client in Pre-contemplation (not ready), Contemplation (thinking about it), or Action (ready to start now)?
 * **Strengths:** "What are you good at? What has worked for you in the past?"
> **Note on Compliance:** For organizations like yours, ensure the intake process aligns with **ASAM Criteria** (American Society of Addiction Medicine) to determine the appropriate level of care—whether it be outpatient, intensive outpatient (IOP), or residential.
